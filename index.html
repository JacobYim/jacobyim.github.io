Understanding reality through digital twins

Start with observations of the real world. Build a digital representation of its changing state, analyze what is happening, and use that understanding to address a physical problem. These five examples illustrate this idea across different scales.

Shared state   Vehicle position · Pedestrian position · Speed · Lanes

01 / Observe reality

Observe vehicle and pedestrian positions, motion, and road geometry.

02 / Represent state

Match vehicle and pedestrian states using the same entity IDs and time reference.

03 / Analyze and update

Evaluate a stopping trajectory in the twin, let the physical vehicle decelerate, and observe the outcome again.

Shared state   Environment geometry · Object position · Joint pose · Contact state

01 / Observe reality

Scan the geometry and object positions of the physical workcell before introducing a robot.

02 / Represent state

Reconstruct the environment first, then introduce a virtual robot to test task poses and trajectories.

03 / Analyze and update

Analyze feasibility and collision risks in the twin, then use the results to solve the physical task.

Shared state   Microstructure · Temperature field · Charge state · Estimation uncertainty

01 / Observe reality

Connect observable thermal and electrical signals with structural information about a battery.

02 / Represent state

Combine measurements and models to estimate internal state. The full interior is not directly observed.

03 / Analyze and update

Analyze thermal and state-change scenarios, then update the estimates with new measurements.

Shared state   Heart motion · Blood flow · Sensor signals · Estimation uncertainty

01 / Observe reality

Observe anatomical structure and changes over time through imaging and physiological signals.

02 / Represent state

Align heart geometry and cardiac phase; estimate unobserved internal states with a model.

03 / Analyze and update

Update simulated heartbeat and blood flow against observed signals. This illustration does not present a diagnosis.

Shared state   Interpersonal relationships · Individual concepts · Different perspectives · Shared understanding

01 / Observe reality

People exchange experiences and perspectives. Observe dialogue, responses, and patterns of interaction.

02 / Represent state

Blue links represent interpersonal relationships; amber graphs represent individual concepts and perspectives inferred from dialogue.

03 / Analyze and update

New explanations and responses update individual conceptual connections and shared understanding, while different perspectives remain distinct.

Conceptual examples illustrated with AI-generated images. These are not experimental results or deployed systems. Internal states and individual perspectives are model-based estimates with uncertainty.
