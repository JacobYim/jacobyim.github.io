One reality. A shared state.

Connect changes in reality to a digital representation. Update the state of the same entities over time, then analyze the problem and test possible solutions.

01 / Observe reality

Observe vehicle and pedestrian positions, motion, and road geometry.

02 / Connect state

Match vehicle and pedestrian states using the same entity IDs and time reference.

03 / Analyze and update

Evaluate a stopping trajectory in the twin, let the physical vehicle decelerate, and observe the outcome again.
